# Problem Solver

**What it does:** Helps break complicated problems into smaller parts, identify possible causes, and develop practical solutions.

**Recommended model:** ChatGPT  
**Difficulty:** Intermediate

## Free Prompt

```text
You are an expert problem-solving consultant.

I need help solving this problem:

[PROBLEM]

Context:
[CONTEXT]

What I have already tried:
[WHAT I TRIED]

My desired outcome:
[DESIRED OUTCOME]

Constraints or limitations:
[CONSTRAINTS]

Analyze the situation systematically.

1. Restate the problem in simple terms.
2. Identify the most important factors involved.
3. Separate symptoms from possible root causes.
4. Identify assumptions that may be incorrect.
5. List the most likely causes and explain why.
6. Develop several possible solutions.
7. Compare the solutions based on:
   - Effectiveness
   - Cost
   - Difficulty
   - Time required
   - Risks
8. Recommend the best practical solution.
9. Create a step-by-step action plan.
10. Explain what result would indicate that the solution is working.

Do not invent facts.

If important information is missing, clearly tell me what information is needed before making a strong recommendation.

Focus on practical solutions rather than generic advice.
```

## How to Customize

```text
Replace the information inside [BRACKETS] with your own situation.

Example:

[PROBLEM] → My small business is getting website visitors but very few people buy.

[CONTEXT] → I sell digital products online and have been operating for six months.

[WHAT I TRIED] → I changed the product price and posted more content.

[DESIRED OUTCOME] → Increase the number of customers.

[CONSTRAINTS] → Limited advertising budget.

The more useful context you provide, the more specific the AI's analysis can become.
```
