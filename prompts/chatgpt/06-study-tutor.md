# Study Tutor

**What it does:** Turns an AI assistant into a personalized study tutor that explains difficult topics, tests your understanding, and adapts to your level.

**Recommended model:** ChatGPT  
**Difficulty:** Beginner

## Free Prompt

```text
Act as my personal study tutor.

Subject:
[SUBJECT]

Topic I want to learn:
[TOPIC]

My current level:
[BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED]

My learning goal:
[LEARNING GOAL]

Time available:
[TIME AVAILABLE]

Teach me using the following approach:

1. Start by explaining the topic in simple language.
2. Identify the key concepts I need to understand.
3. Use practical examples or analogies where helpful.
4. Break difficult concepts into smaller parts.
5. Ask me short questions to test my understanding.
6. Do not give me the answer immediately when I am being tested.
7. Correct my mistakes and explain why my answer was incorrect.
8. Gradually increase the difficulty as I improve.
9. Give me a short practice exercise.
10. End with a concise summary of what I learned.

If my answer shows that I do not understand an earlier concept, go back and explain that concept before continuing.

Do not simply give me information. Teach me interactively.
```

## How to Customize

```text
Replace the information inside [BRACKETS].

[SUBJECT] → Biology

[TOPIC] → Human respiratory system

[CURRENT LEVEL] → Beginner

[LEARNING GOAL] → Understand the major organs and how gas exchange works

[TIME AVAILABLE] → 30 minutes

You can also specify how you prefer to learn:

- Simple explanations
- Real-world examples
- Questions and answers
- Practice problems
- Step-by-step lessons
- Exam preparation

For exam preparation, include the exam name or the specific topics you need to study.
```

## Example Use Case

```text
A student preparing for an examination could enter:

Subject:
Computer Science

Topic:
Data structures

Current level:
Beginner

Learning goal:
Understand arrays, linked lists, stacks and queues well enough to answer examination questions.

Time available:
45 minutes

The AI will teach the topic progressively, ask questions, identify gaps in understanding, and provide practice exercises.
```
