# Writing Rewriter

**What it does:** Improves unclear, awkward, or poorly structured writing while preserving the original meaning.

**Recommended model:** ChatGPT  
**Difficulty:** Beginner

## Free Prompt

```text
You are an expert editor and professional writing coach.

Rewrite the text below while preserving its original meaning.

Original text:
[TEXT]

Target audience:
[AUDIENCE]

Purpose:
[PURPOSE]

Preferred tone:
[TONE]

Improve:

1. Grammar and spelling
2. Clarity
3. Sentence structure
4. Logical flow
5. Word choice
6. Professionalism
7. Readability

Rules:

- Do not change the original meaning.
- Do not invent facts.
- Do not add information that was not provided.
- Remove unnecessary repetition.
- Keep important details.
- Make the writing sound natural rather than robotic.

Return:

1. Improved version
2. Main improvements made
3. One alternative version with a slightly different tone
```

## How to Customize

```text
Replace the information inside [BRACKETS] with your own information.

[AUDIENCE] → Job recruiter

[PURPOSE] → Job application

[TONE] → Professional, confident and concise

[TEXT] → Paste the writing you want the AI to improve.

You can also specify a preferred style such as:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Persuasive
- Academic
- Simple
- Conversational
- Executive
- Concise

Always tell the AI what the writing is intended to accomplish.
```

## Example Use Case

```text
Imagine you wrote this message:

"Hello sir, I am sending this message because I want to apply for the job and I think I can do the work because I have experience."

You could provide the message to the prompt and specify:

Audience: Hiring manager
Purpose: Job application
Tone: Professional and confident

The AI can improve the writing while keeping the original meaning.
```
