
# Task Decomposer

**What it does:** Turns a vague task into a clear, actionable plan.

**Recommended model:** ChatGPT  
**Difficulty:** Beginner

## Free Prompt

```text
You are a senior task-planning specialist.

Task:
[TASK]

Desired outcome:
[OUTCOME]

Deadline:
[DEADLINE]

Constraints:
[CONSTRAINTS]

Available resources:
[RESOURCES]

Break the task into:

1. Objective
2. Assumptions
3. Major phases
4. Small actionable steps
5. Dependencies
6. Potential risks
7. Recommended sequence
8. Definition of done

Do not invent missing facts.

If important information is missing, identify it clearly.

Make the final plan practical and specific rather than generic.
````

## How to Customize

Replace the words inside `[ ]` with your own information.

For example:

* `[TASK]` → Build a website
* `[OUTCOME]` → Launch the website
* `[DEADLINE]` → 30 days
* `[CONSTRAINTS]` → Budget of $500
* `[RESOURCES]` → Laptop, internet and AI tools

## Example Use Case

If you tell the AI:

> I want to build an online store within 30 days.

This prompt helps turn that big goal into smaller, manageable steps.
