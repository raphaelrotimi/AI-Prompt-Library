# Email Professionalizer

**What it does:** Turns a rough email or message into a clear, professional, natural-sounding email without changing the intended meaning.

**Recommended model:** ChatGPT  
**Difficulty:** Beginner

## Free Prompt

```text
You are an expert professional communication editor.

Rewrite the email below so it is clear, professional, natural, and easy to understand.

Original email:
[EMAIL]

Recipient:
[RECIPIENT]

Purpose of the email:
[PURPOSE]

Preferred tone:
[TONE]

Important details that must remain:
[IMPORTANT DETAILS]

Requirements:

1. Preserve the original meaning.
2. Do not invent facts or promises.
3. Correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
4. Remove unnecessary repetition.
5. Improve clarity and structure.
6. Make the message respectful and confident.
7. Keep the tone appropriate for the recipient.
8. Do not make the email unnecessarily long.
9. If the original message is unclear, identify the unclear part instead of guessing.

Return:

Subject:
[WRITE A CLEAR SUBJECT]

Email:
[REWRITTEN EMAIL]

Briefly explain the major improvements you made.
```

## How to Customize

```text
Replace the information inside [BRACKETS].

[EMAIL] → Paste your original email.

[RECIPIENT] → Hiring manager, client, landlord, colleague, professor, customer, etc.

[PURPOSE] → Job application, complaint, request, follow-up, proposal, apology, inquiry, etc.

[TONE] → Professional, friendly, firm, respectful, persuasive, concise, etc.

[IMPORTANT DETAILS] → Any information that must not be removed from the email.

For best results, tell the AI exactly who will receive the message and what you want the recipient to do after reading it.
```

## Example Use Case

```text
You received an email from a potential client and want to respond professionally.

You can provide:

Recipient:
Potential client

Purpose:
Follow up after a business discussion

Tone:
Professional and friendly

Important details:
Mention the agreed meeting date and confirm the next step.

Then paste your rough email into [EMAIL].

The prompt will help turn the rough message into a polished professional email while preserving the important information.
```
